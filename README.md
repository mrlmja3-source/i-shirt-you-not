## 💥 What is this?

**I Shirt You Not** is a brand built around one idea:

> If it’s funny, real, or a little savage… it belongs on a shirt.

No corporate cringe.  
No fake inspirational nonsense.  
Just designs that actually hit.

---

## 🎯 Why this exists

Most shirts are:
- Generic 😴  
- Overused 🥱  
- Trying way too hard 🤡  

We’re not doing that.

We make stuff people actually want to wear in real life.
